# Drop terms that do no work

A term that does not improve the description adds uncertainty and invites a false story.

The NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook states: "However, if enough extra terms are left in the model, the consequences can be serious." ([How can I test whether all of the terms in the functional part of the model are necessary?](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/pmd/section4/pmd447.htm), checked 2026-10-17.)

## What to do

- Remove it when the comparison shows it is not needed.
- Do not remove a term the subject knowledge requires just because the sample is small.
- Say which comparison justified the removal.
