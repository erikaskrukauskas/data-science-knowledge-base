# Optimize inside the observed region

An optimum found from a fitted surface is a claim about the region you studied.

The NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook states: "The example discussed below includes a graphical depiction of the optimization process." ([Optimization](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/pmd/section1/pmd134.htm), checked 2026-10-17.)

## What to do

- Do not move the recommended setting outside that region and keep the fitted uncertainty.
- If the surface is a plane with no curvature, the optimum will sit on the boundary. Say that.
- Confirm a recommended setting with runs at that setting.
