# Calibrate before you interpret

Calibration connects an instrument reading to the reference scale.

The NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook states: "Calibration from first principles of physics and reciprocity calibration are not discussed." ([Issues in calibration](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/mpc/section3/mpc31.htm), checked 2026-10-17.)

## What to do

- Interpret study measurements on the calibrated scale.
- An uncalibrated reading compared across instruments mixes instrument bias into the result.
- Say the range the calibration covers. Outside that range you are extrapolating.
