# Compare two groups by rank when the shape is unknown

A rank comparison of two groups asks whether one tends to produce larger values, and it does not estimate a difference of means.

The NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook states: "The alternative hypothesis is: The central tendencies are NOT the same." ([Do two arbitrary processes have the same central tendency?](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section3/prc35.htm), checked 2026-10-19.)

## What to do

- Use it when you will not assume a shape.
- Say that the result is a shift in the ranks.
- If the decision needs a difference in original units, a rank test is only the first check.
