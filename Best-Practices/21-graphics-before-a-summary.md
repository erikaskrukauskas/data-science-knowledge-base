# Graph before you summarize

A single number hides shape, clumps, and points that sit apart.

The NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook states: "Graphical procedures are not just tools that we could use in an EDA context, they are tools that we must use." ([The Role of Graphics](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/eda/section1/eda15.htm), checked 2026-10-17.)

## What to do

- Look at a plot of the measurements before you quote a mean, a rate, or a fit summary.
- If the plot shows more than one cluster, do not describe them with one center.
- Say which plot you looked at when the summary is the result you report.
