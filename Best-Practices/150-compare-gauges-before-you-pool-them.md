# Compare gauges before you pool their measurements

Measurements from different gauges can be pooled only after you have looked at the differences among the gauges.

The NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook states: "Gauges that are consistently above or below the other gauges are biased high or low, respectively, relative to the average." ([Differences among gauges](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/mpc/section4/mpc454.htm), checked 2026-10-19.)

## What to do

- Name the gauges and the reference they were compared with.
- Keep a gauge offset in the analysis when the gauges disagree by enough to matter.
- Do not treat gauge identity as a harmless label.
