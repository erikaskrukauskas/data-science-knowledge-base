# Choose the sampling plan from the risks

A single sampling plan is a sample size and an accept number.

The NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook states: "Two methods for choosing a single sample acceptance plan A single sampling plan, as previously defined , is specified by the pair of numbers ( n,c )." ([How do you Choose a Single Sampling Plan?](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/pmc/section2/pmc23.htm), checked 2026-10-17.)

## What to do

- Choose them from the risks you will tolerate at the quality levels that matter.
- A plan copied from another product carries that product's risks.
- Say the quality level the plan is built to catch.
