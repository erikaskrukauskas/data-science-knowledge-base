# Match the sample to the process

A sampling plan states which units, which times, and which conditions enter the study.

The NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook states: "design a sampling scheme that details how and when samples will be taken ." ([Define Sampling Plan](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/ppc/section3/ppc33.htm), checked 2026-10-17.)

## What to do

- If the plan misses a shift, a line, or a period the decision cares about, the result does not cover that part.
- Say what the plan left out.
- A convenient stretch of steady running is a result about steady running.
