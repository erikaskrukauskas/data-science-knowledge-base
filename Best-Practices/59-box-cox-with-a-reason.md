# Transform with a stated reason

A Box-Cox plot suggests a power that straightens a relationship or stabilizes spread.

The NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook states: "The Box-Cox transformation is a particularly useful family of transformations." ([Box-Cox Linearity Plot](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/eda/section3/eda335.htm), checked 2026-10-17.)

## What to do

- Transform for that stated reason.
- Report the result on the scale the decision uses, and say the fit was on the transformed scale.
- Do not search powers until a p-value clears a hurdle.
