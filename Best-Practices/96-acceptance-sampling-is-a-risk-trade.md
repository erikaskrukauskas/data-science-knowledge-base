# Treat acceptance sampling as a risk trade

Acceptance sampling decides whether a lot meets a quality level by measuring a sample, not every unit.

The NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook states: "This process is called Lot Acceptance Sampling or just Acceptance Sampling ." ([What is Acceptance Sampling?](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/pmc/section2/pmc21.htm), checked 2026-10-17.)

## What to do

- There is a chance of accepting a bad lot and of rejecting a good one.
- Name those risks before you choose the plan.
- A sample that happened to look clean is not proof the lot is clean.
