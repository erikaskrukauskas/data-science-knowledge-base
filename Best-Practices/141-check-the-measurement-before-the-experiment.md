# Check the measurement before the experiment

An experiment cannot see a factor effect that is smaller than the measurement variation, so the measurement has to be checked before the runs are spent.

The NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook states: "Strange experimental outcomes can often be traced to `hiccups' in the metrology system." ([Is the measurement system capable?](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/pri/section2/pri21.htm), checked 2026-10-19.)

## What to do

- Compare the measurement spread with the effect size you need to detect.
- If the measurement cannot resolve that effect, fix the measurement or change the question.
- Do not interpret a flat experiment as proof that the factors do nothing until the measurement is ruled out.
