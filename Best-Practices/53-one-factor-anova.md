# Compare several groups as one question

When more than two groups are compared, the first question is whether the group means differ at all.

The NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook states: "One-Way ANOVA Example A one-way analysis of variance was generated for the GEAR." ([One-Factor ANOVA](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/eda/section3/eda354.htm), checked 2026-10-17.)

## What to do

- An analysis of variance addresses that question under its assumptions.
- Looking at every pair afterwards is a further question, with a further penalty for the number of looks.
- Do not report the most different pair as if it had been the only comparison.
