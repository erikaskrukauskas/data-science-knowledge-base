# Analyze paired observations as pairs

Paired observations are compared within the pair, and an unpaired two-sample analysis throws away the pairing and answers a wider question.

The NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook states: "The paired-sample t test is used to test for the difference of two means before and after a treatment." ([Analysis of paired observations](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section3/prc311.htm), checked 2026-10-19.)

## What to do

- Say what makes the pair: the same unit before and after, or two units matched on purpose.
- Analyze the within-pair differences.
- Do not switch to an unpaired test because the unpaired result looks stronger.
