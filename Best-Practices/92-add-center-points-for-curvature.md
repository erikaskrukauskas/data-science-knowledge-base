# Add center points to see curvature

Center points, at the middle of the factor ranges, show whether the response bends.

The NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook states: "The number of center points can make the design orthogonal or have 'uniform precision." ([Adding centerpoints](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/pri/section3/pri337.htm), checked 2026-10-17.)

## What to do

- A two-level design without them cannot see curvature.
- If the center differs from the average of the corners, a flat model is incomplete.
- Spend a few runs on the center before you recommend a setting in the middle.
