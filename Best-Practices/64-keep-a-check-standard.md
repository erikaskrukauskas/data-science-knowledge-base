# Keep a check standard

A check standard is a stable item measured repeatedly so the measurement process can be watched.

The NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook states: "Time-dependent sources of error are evaluated and quantified from the database of check standard measurements." ([What is a check standard?](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/mpc/section1/mpc12.htm), checked 2026-10-17.)

## What to do

- Use it to see drift and day-to-day movement.
- A check standard is not a substitute for measuring the units in the decision.
- If the check standard moves, later measurements of the study units are suspect until the process is brought back.
