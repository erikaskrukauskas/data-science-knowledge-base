# Chart the measurement process

A Shewhart chart on a check standard asks whether the measurement process has shifted.

The NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook states: "Example of Shewhart control chart for mass calibrations The Shewhart control chart has a baseline and upper and lower limits, shown as dashed lines, that are symmetric about the baseline." ([Shewhart control chart](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/mpc/section2/mpc221.htm), checked 2026-10-17.)

## What to do

- A point beyond the limits, or a run of points on one side, is a signal to stop and find the cause.
- Do not keep reporting study measurements through an unnoticed shift.
- The chart limits come from the stable process, not from the latest inconvenience.
