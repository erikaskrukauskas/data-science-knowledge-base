# Scale factors to the region you will run

Coded factor levels describe the region of the experiment, and an analysis in coded units still has to be translated back to the process units you will set.

The NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook states: "The use of +1 and -1 for the factor settings is called coding the data." ([How do you select and scale the process variables?](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/pri/section3/pri32.htm), checked 2026-10-19.)

## What to do

- State the low and high of each factor in process units.
- Say whether the fitted equation is in coded units or original units.
- Do not compare coefficients across factors unless the coding is the same.
