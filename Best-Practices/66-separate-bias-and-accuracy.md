# Separate bias from accuracy

Accuracy includes both the systematic shift and the scatter. Bias is the shift.

The NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook states: "Definition of Accuracy and Bias Accuracy is a qualitative term referring to whether there is agreement between a measurement made on an object and its true (target or reference) value." ([Bias and Accuracy](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/mpc/section1/mpc113.htm), checked 2026-10-17.)

## What to do

- A precise process can still be inaccurate if it sits away from the reference.
- Correct or report the bias. Do not quote precision as accuracy.
- Say which part of the error the reported uncertainty includes.
