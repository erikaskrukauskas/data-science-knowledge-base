# Estimate repeatability from repeats under the same conditions

Repeatability is the spread of repeats under conditions that were held fixed, and it does not include operator, day, or laboratory differences.

The NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook states: "Fortunately, these standard deviations can be pooled over days; runs; and check standards, if appropriate, to produce a more reliable precision measure." ([Analysis of repeatability](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/mpc/section4/mpc441.htm), checked 2026-10-19.)

## What to do

- State which conditions were held fixed.
- Do not call a multi-day or multi-operator spread a repeatability.
- Use a wider reproducibility figure when the result has to travel across those conditions.
