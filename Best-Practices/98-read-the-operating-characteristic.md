# Read the operating characteristic of the plan

The operating-characteristic curve is the chance the plan accepts a lot, across quality levels.

The NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook states: "The OC curve for a (52, 3) sampling plan is shown below." ([Choosing a Sampling Plan with a given OC Curve](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/pmc/section2/pmc232.htm), checked 2026-10-17.)

## What to do

- Read it before you adopt the plan.
- Two plans with the same sample size can accept bad lots at very different rates.
- The curve is the check that the plan matches the risk.
