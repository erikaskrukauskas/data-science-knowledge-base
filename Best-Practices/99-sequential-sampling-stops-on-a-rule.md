# Stop a sequential sample on a pre-set rule

A sequential plan lets you stop early when the evidence is already strong, under limits fixed in advance.

The NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook states: "Sequential Sampling Sequential sampling is different from single, double or multiple sampling." ([What is a Sequential Sampling Plan?](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/pmc/section2/pmc26.htm), checked 2026-10-17.)

## What to do

- Looking, then deciding whether to continue because the result is awkward, is not that plan.
- The stopping rule is part of the design.
- Report the rule and the sample size it produced.
