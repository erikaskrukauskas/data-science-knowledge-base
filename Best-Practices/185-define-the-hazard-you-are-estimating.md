# Define the hazard you are estimating

The hazard is the instantaneous failure rate among units still at risk, and a single average failure rate hides whether that risk rises or falls with age.

The NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook states: "It is a rate per unit of time similar in meaning to reading a car speedometer at a particular instant and seeing 45 mph." ([Failure (or hazard) rate](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/apr/section1/apr123.htm), checked 2026-10-19.)

## What to do

- Say whether you are reporting a constant hazard or a hazard that changes with age.
- Plot or model the hazard against age when the decision depends on wear-out or infant failure.
- Do not quote one average rate for a mechanism whose risk changes with age.
