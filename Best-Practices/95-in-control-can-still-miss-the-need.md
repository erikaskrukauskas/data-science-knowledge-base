# An in-control process can still miss the need

A process can be stable and still produce too many results outside the limits the decision cares about.

The NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook states: "What do you do if the process is ï¿½in controlï¿½ but the average level is too high or too low or the variability is unacceptable?" ([What to do if "In Control" but Unacceptable?](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/pmc/section1/pmc15.htm), checked 2026-10-17.)

## What to do

- Control means predictable. It does not mean acceptable.
- If the stable process misses the need, change the process. Do not tighten the chart and hope.
- Say which judgment you are making.
