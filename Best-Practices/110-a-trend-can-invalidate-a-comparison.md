# A trend can invalidate a pooled comparison

A trend over the collection window means the early and late measurements are not replicates of one population.

The NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook states: "If the line looks horizontal, then there is no evidence of a trend; otherwise there is." ([What are trends in sequential process or product data?](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section1/prc17.htm), checked 2026-10-17.)

## What to do

- A pooled comparison then mixes the trend into the group difference.
- Show the sequence.
- Compare within periods, or model the trend, before you claim a group effect.
