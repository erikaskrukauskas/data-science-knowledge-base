# Check gauge linearity across the range you will use

A gauge bias that changes across the measured range is a linearity problem, and a single-point check will not show it.

The NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook states: "If the slope is different from one, the gauge is non-linear and requires calibration or repair." ([Linearity of the gauge](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/mpc/section4/mpc452.htm), checked 2026-10-19.)

## What to do

- Compare the gauge with a reference at several points across the range you will report.
- State whether the bias is constant or whether it grows with the level.
- Do not apply one correction factor across a range where the bias changes.
