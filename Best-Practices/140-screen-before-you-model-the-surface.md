# Screen before you model the surface

A screening design exists to drop factors, and it is the wrong design for the final surface once the active factors are known.

The NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook states: "The term 'Screening Design' refers to an experimental plan that is intended to find the few significant factors from a list of many potential ones." ([Screening designs](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/pri/section3/pri3346.htm), checked 2026-10-19.)

## What to do

- State that the purpose is to find which factors can be set aside.
- Move the factors you keep into a design that can estimate the interactions and curvature you now care about.
- Do not publish the screening equation as the process model.

This page is the reusable rule. Dataset names, thresholds, and the numeric result stay in the project.
