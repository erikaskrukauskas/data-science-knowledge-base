# Fold a fraction over to clear an alias you now care about

A foldover adds the mirror fraction so a chosen alias, often a main effect with two-factor interactions, can be separated.

The NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook states: "That is, we can estimate all the main effects clear of any two-factor interaction." ([Mirror-Image foldover designs](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/pri/section3/pri3381.htm), checked 2026-10-19.)

## What to do

- Name the effect you are trying to free before you add the new runs.
- A foldover does not clear every alias. Read the new resolution and the remaining aliases.
- Do not pretend the first fraction alone identified an interaction it was aliased with.
