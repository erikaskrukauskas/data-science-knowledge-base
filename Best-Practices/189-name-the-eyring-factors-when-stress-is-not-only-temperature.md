# Name the Eyring factors when the stress is not temperature alone

An Eyring model is a different acceleration equation from Arrhenius, and it is used when the stress mechanism is the one that model describes.

The NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook states: "Many well-known models are simplified versions of the Eyring model with appropriate functions of relevant stresses chosen for S_1 and S_2 ." ([Eyring](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/apr/section1/apr152.htm), checked 2026-10-19.)

## What to do

- Write the stresses in the model and the use condition for each.
- Do not swap in an Eyring equation because an Arrhenius fit was inconvenient.
- Show the extrapolation distance from the tested stresses to use.
