# Report a repair rate for a system that is fixed and returned

The rate of occurrence of failures is the right summary for a system that is repaired and kept in service, and a mean time to first failure is a different summary.

The NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook states: "A different approach is used for modeling the rate of occurrence of failure incidences for a repairable system." ([Repair rate or ROCOF](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/apr/section1/apr125.htm), checked 2026-10-19.)

## What to do

- Count recurrent events on systems that stay in service.
- Say whether the rate is treated as constant or allowed to change.
- Do not convert a repair rate into a life-distribution percentile without a model that justifies it.
