# Confirm a designed result with new runs

A fitted design model is confirmed by new runs at the chosen conditions, not by the residuals of the runs that built the model.

The NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook states: "The confirmation runs should be conducted in an environment as similar as possible to the original experiment." ([How to confirm DOE results (confirmatory runs)](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/pri/section4/pri46.htm), checked 2026-10-19.)

## What to do

- Pick the confirmation points from the conclusion you intend to use.
- Compare the new observations with the prediction interval, not with the fitted value alone.
- A miss on the confirmation runs sends you back to the model. It does not get averaged away.
