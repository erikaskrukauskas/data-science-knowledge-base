# Skip lots only after a history you stated

Skip-lot sampling inspects some lots and skips others only after a stated history of accepted lots, and it changes the protection of the plan.

The NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook states: "However skip-lot sampling should only be used when it has been demonstrated that the quality of the submitted product is very good." ([What is Skip Lot Sampling?](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/pmc/section2/pmc27.htm), checked 2026-10-19.)

## What to do

- Write the qualification history and the skip frequency before you skip.
- A failure during skip status returns you to the tighter plan. It does not get one more skip.
- Do not skip lots because inspection is behind.
