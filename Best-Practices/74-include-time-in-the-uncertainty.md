# Include time-dependent error in the uncertainty

Time is a carrier of measurement error: environment and handling change.

The NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook states: "The uncertainty statement is not 'true' to its purpose if it describes a situation that cannot be reproduced over time." ([Type A evaluations of time-dependent effects](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/mpc/section5/mpc5311.htm), checked 2026-10-17.)

## What to do

- If the reported value is meant to cover more than one day, the uncertainty has to include that movement.
- A same-day standard deviation understates it.
- Match the time span of the repeats to the time span of the claim.
