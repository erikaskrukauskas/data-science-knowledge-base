# Keep quality and reliability as different questions

Quality at the point of manufacture and reliability over life are different questions, and a pass at shipment does not estimate a lifetime.

The NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook states: "Quality is a snapshot at the start of life and reliability is a motion picture of the day-by-day operation." ([Quality versus reliability](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/apr/section1/apr111.htm), checked 2026-10-19.)

## What to do

- Say whether the decision is about the unit as made, or about the unit after time in use.
- Use a lifetime or degradation analysis for the second question.
- Do not report a manufacturing yield as a reliability.
