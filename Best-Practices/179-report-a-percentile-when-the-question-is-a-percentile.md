# Report a percentile when the question is a percentile

A percentile is the value that a stated share of the population falls at or below, and the mean is not that value once the distribution is skewed.

The NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook states: "This percentile, although it is an estimate from a small sample of resistivities measurements, gives an indication of the percentile for a population of resistivity measurements." ([Percentiles](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section2/prc262.htm), checked 2026-10-19.)

## What to do

- Name the percentile and the population.
- Say how it was estimated and give it an interval.
- Do not describe the mean as the 90th percentile of the units.
