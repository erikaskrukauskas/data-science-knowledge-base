# Keep the optimum inside the experimental region

An optimum subject to the experimental region is allowed only at conditions you could have run.

The NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook states: "Optimal operating conditions may fall outside region where experiment conducted Sometimes the optimal operating conditions x * simply fall outside the region where the experiment was conducted." ([Single response: Optimization subject to experimental region constraints](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/pri/section5/pri5316.htm), checked 2026-10-17.)

## What to do

- A stationary point outside that region is a direction, not a demonstrated best setting.
- Recommend a setting you can point to on the design.
- Confirm it with runs.
