# Compare several proportions as a family

Comparing several proportions in pairs is a family of comparisons, and each pair has to be read as part of that family.

The NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook states: "The Marascuilo procedure enables us to simultaneously test the differences of all pairs of proportions when there are several populations under investigation." ([Comparing multiple proportions: The Marascuillo procedure](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section4/prc474.htm), checked 2026-10-19.)

## What to do

- Name every proportion in the family before you highlight a pair.
- Use a procedure that keeps the family error in view.
- A single pair that looks different is not a conclusion until the family is accounted for.
