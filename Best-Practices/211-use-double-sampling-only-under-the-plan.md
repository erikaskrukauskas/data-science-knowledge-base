# Use double sampling only under a plan written in advance

Double sampling allows a second sample only when the first sample falls in a continue region that was fixed in advance.

The NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook states: "For example, if in double sampling the results of the first sample are not conclusive with regard to accepting or rejecting, a second sample is taken." ([What is Double Sampling?](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/pmc/section2/pmc24.htm), checked 2026-10-19.)

## What to do

- Write the first-sample accept and reject numbers, and the second-sample rule, before you inspect.
- The second sample is not a chance to keep looking until the lot passes.
- Read the operating characteristic of the whole plan, not of the first sample alone.
