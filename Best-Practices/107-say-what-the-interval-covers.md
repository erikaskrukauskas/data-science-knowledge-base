# Say what the confidence interval covers

A confidence interval is a range, built by a stated method, for a parameter of a stated population.

The NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook states: "More information on confidence intervals can also be found in Chapter 1 ." ([What are confidence intervals?](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section1/prc14.htm), checked 2026-10-17.)

## What to do

- It is not a range that contains most of the individual measurements, unless you built a different interval for that purpose.
- Say the population, the parameter, and the assumptions.
- An interval from a sample that does not represent the population does not cover that population.
