# Account for multiple comparisons

Each additional comparison raises the chance of highlighting a gap that is only sampling noise.

The NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook states: "However, there are also several powerful multiple comparison procedures we can use after observing the experimental results." ([How can we make multiple comparisons?](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section4/prc47.htm), checked 2026-10-17.)

## What to do

- If you look at many pairs, use a procedure that accounts for the family of looks, or treat the highlighted pair as exploratory.
- A pre-declared single comparison does not pay that penalty.
- Say how many comparisons were open when you chose the one to report.
