# Compare a proportion with a requirement on the proportion scale

A requirement on a proportion of defectives is a statement about a binomial rate, and a mean of the raw observations does not answer it.

The NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook states: "The relevant test is the one-sided test which guards against an increase in proportion defective from its historical level." ([Does the proportion of defectives meet requirements?](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section2/prc24.htm), checked 2026-10-19.)

## What to do

- Define what counts as a defective and the set of units the proportion refers to.
- Put an interval on the proportion.
- State the requirement on that same scale before you say the process meets it.
