# Compare two proportions as proportions

Two processes are compared on their proportions of defectives with a procedure for proportions, not with a test meant for means of continuous measurements.

The NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook states: "By "more extreme", we mean relative to the null hypothesis of equal proportions." ([How can we determine whether two processes produce the same proportion of defectives?](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section3/prc33.htm), checked 2026-10-19.)

## What to do

- Use the same definition of a defective for both processes.
- Report the two proportions and an interval for their difference.
- Check that the sample sizes support the approximation you are using.
