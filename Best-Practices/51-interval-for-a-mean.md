# Pair a mean with an interval

A mean of a sample is an estimate. The interval says how far that estimate might sit from the mean of the population the sample represents.

The NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook states: "If the confidence interval contains 5, then H 0 cannot be rejected." ([Confidence Limits for the Mean](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/eda/section3/eda352.htm), checked 2026-10-17.)

## What to do

- State the population and the assumptions.
- Do not quote the mean as if the sample were the population.
- A narrow interval from dependent measurements is not as informative as the formula suggests.
