# Establish stability before capability

Stability means the process distribution is not shifting while you measure it.

The NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook states: "Graphical methods usually good enough Typically, graphical methods are good enough for evaluating process stability." ([Assessing Process Stability](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/ppc/section4/ppc45.htm), checked 2026-10-17.)

## What to do

- A capability number compares that distribution with limits. Without stability, the comparison describes a moving target.
- Show the time plot or the chart before the capability figure.
- Do not quote capability for a period that contains a known shift.
