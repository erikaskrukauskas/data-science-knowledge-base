# Compare groups with a box plot

Compare groups by their centers, their spreads, and their separated points, not by their means alone.

The NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook states: "For a single box plot, the width of the box is arbitrary." ([Box Plot](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/eda/section3/eda337.htm), checked 2026-10-17.)

## What to do

- A box plot shows whether the groups overlap.
- A mean difference with heavy overlap is a weak distinction.
- Points that sit apart belong in the description of the group.
