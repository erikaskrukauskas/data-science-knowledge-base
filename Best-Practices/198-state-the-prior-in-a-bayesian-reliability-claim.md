# State the prior in a Bayesian reliability claim

A Bayesian reliability number combines the current test with a prior, and the prior is part of the claim.

The NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook states: "In particular, conjugate priors (defined below) are a natural and popular choice of Bayesian prior distribution models." ([How can Bayesian methodology be used for reliability evaluation?](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/apr/section1/apr1a.htm), checked 2026-10-19.)

## What to do

- Write the prior and what evidence or engineering judgment it represents.
- Show how much the posterior moved from the prior.
- Do not report the posterior as if it were a summary of the current test alone.
