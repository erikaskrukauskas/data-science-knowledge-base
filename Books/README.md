# Books and references

This folder is a catalog of data-science references and original notes about them. It is not a library of book files.

## What to store

- Title, author, edition, and year.
- The analysis question the source helps answer.
- Original notes in your own words.
- A short quotation only when you need the exact wording, with a citation.

## What to leave out

- PDFs and ebook files. `.gitignore` ignores `*.pdf` as a backstop.
- Verbatim chapters or reconstructed passages.
- Scanned pages and bulk exports from a reading tool.

AI-tooling references belong in the AI knowledge base. Application, pipeline, and release references belong in those domain repos, under the same note-taking rule.

## Using a book in an analysis

Search your notes. Then check the current official page before you apply a claim about a method's present definition or a library feature. A book can explain a durable measurement idea and still disagree with the page you will rely on today.
